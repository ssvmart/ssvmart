About SSV MART

Located at Vishnupriya Commercial Complex, Bonthapadu Bypass, Near TSR Ginning Mill in Guntur, SSV MART is dedicated to delivering superior quality yarns, woven fabrics, and packaging solutions. Guided by our core principle "The Right Fit", every spool of thread, roll of woven fabric, and heavy-duty sack we offer is carefully selected for lasting strength, uniform weave, and dependable performance.

We serve agricultural traders, textile mills, commercial packaging units, and retail businesses with flexible order quantities and prompt service. With multiple specifications, vibrant colors, and custom sizing options available across our entire catalog, SSV MART makes sourcing quality yarn and fabric effortless and reliable.
